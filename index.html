The prompt template

Four labeled parts. Copy the structure, replace the brackets, use it for any task. That is the whole method.

The structure

Copy and fill in

Keep the four headings in this order every time. One or two short sentences under each is enough.

CONTEXT: [Your situation and the background that matters]
ROLE: Act as [type of expert or helper]
GOAL: [Exactly what you want made or answered]
CONSTRAINTS: [Length, format, tone, and limits]

Context + Role + Goal + Constraints = a prompt the AI can follow without guessing.

What each part does

Why each part is there

1

Context

What is the situation?

Your subject, level, time, and background.

Why it helps: The answer fits your real situation instead of a generic one.

2

Role

Who should the AI act like?

A teacher, coach, editor, or other helpful perspective.

Why it helps: It sets the tone, level, and kind of advice you get.

3

Goal

What do you want it to do?

The exact task and result you need.

Why it helps: It gives the AI a clear target, so it does not wander or add extras.

4

Constraints

What rules should it follow?

Length, format, tone, time, or things to avoid. Called Rules in the beginner guide.

Why it helps: The result comes back in a form you can actually use.

Why the structure works

Why using a template helps at all

  • No guessing: each heading forces the one detail the AI would otherwise have to invent.
  • Nothing forgotten: the checklist is built in. If a section is empty, you can see what is missing before you send.
  • Faster to write: you fill in blanks instead of starting from a blank page every time.
  • Easier to fix: if the answer is off, change just one part, usually Goal or Constraints, and resend.
  • Reusable everywhere: same four parts work for study plans, emails, recipes, explanations, and plans.
